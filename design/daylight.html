Calendar app · Daylight, revised

Daylight, with the purple taken out and the day put back in.

Same idea as before: the grid is lit by the hour, scheduled work is a silhouette cut into the light, and open time is just where nothing is blocking the sun. The type is now Ballast's, so Bricolage Grotesque carries the numbers and Newsreader carries the task names.

The light follows a real July day in Denver. Sunrise a little before six, the flat bright middle, low sun through leaves from about five, sunset around eight twenty, then green dusk. Shoreline is the chosen ground. The other two are still here to compare against.

What changed

No purple anywhere. The old version ran indigo to rose. This runs cool green dawn, through warm daylight, into amber, then dusk. Every ground is a dark natural colour rather than a night sky.

Bricolage Grotesque and Newsreader, lifted straight from Ballast. Every number is Bricolage, every task name is Newsreader.

The day starts at 5:30am now, since that is when the gym with Ty starts. The old mockup began at seven and had nowhere to put it.

Light through leaves. There is a soft dapple over the whole track, so the bright hours are not a flat wash.
